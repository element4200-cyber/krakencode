Meet the kraken

KrakenCode is a browser-based explorer of the crypto web. The kraken is its digital mascot. Its activity log shows real requests made by your browser.

Track a token

Paste a Solana contract address into the token field. KrakenCode retrieves its indexed pairs, shows price, market cap and volume, and gives you links to DEX Screener and pump.fun. Your last CA stays in this browser. A new token may not have an indexed pair yet.

Find movers

The movers feed combines recent DEX Screener token profiles and boosted tokens with pump.fun and PumpSwap search results. It includes Solana pairs identified by a pump.fun address suffix, pump.fun link, or pump.fun / PumpSwap market. This is a sample, not a complete leaderboard. One pair per token is shown, prioritizing liquidity, then volume. Refreshes every 60 seconds while the page is visible.

Explore the web

Enter a search phrase to search DuckDuckGo through Jina Reader, or paste a public HTTPS URL to retrieve readable page text. Click “read page” on a result. Public services can rate-limit requests or block pages; errors appear in the interface, with a link to open the original source.

How crawling works

While this page is open, the crawler reads a small set of public crypto documentation pages, one at a time, every 45 seconds. Use “pause crawler” to stop it. Collected text is saved locally in this browser, up to 20 pages. No server crawls in the background after you close this page.

Data & privacy

Token lookups go to DEX Screener. Search phrases and URLs are sent to Jina Reader; search uses DuckDuckGo. Don't enter private links or access tokens. No wallet connection is needed. KrakenCode does not train a model, issue a token, manage a treasury, distribute fees, or execute trades.
