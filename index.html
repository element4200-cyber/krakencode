Scan a token

Paste a Solana CA or a pump.fun token URL into the top scanner. KrakenCode chooses the indexed pair with the most liquidity, shows its live DEX Screener chart and descriptive market feedback, and records real price samples in this browser. The selected token and movers refresh every 15 seconds while the tab is visible; pending requests and service limits can delay updates. An unindexed token has no chart or market assessment yet, with a link to its original pump.fun page. The project CA above the scanner comes from config.js; visitor lookups do not change the project CA.

Read a website

Search for a topic or paste a public HTTPS URL. Choose a result to fetch readable page text and an extractive summary. The “live source / chart” button loads the original page in the scan window with the kraken overlay. Some sites block embeds or reading; scanned text and an original-page link are provided when possible. Cross-origin frame content is not accessible to the scanner. Web feedback uses the separately retrieved text; token feedback uses API metrics, not chart pixel interpretation.

Chat with the reader

Paste information to read it locally, send a CA or URL for a lookup, or use “search …” for web results. Ask “summarize”, “risks”, or a question about the currently scanned source to retrieve passages or its market checks. Responses are extractive summaries and rule-based checks, not an AI language model or independent fact-check. This is an individual session, not a shared visitor chat room. Up to 30 messages and 10 pasted sources stay in memory and are removed on reload.

Automatic scan cycle

The scan rotates every 20 seconds through the top 10 currently ranked movers, collected webpages plus seed documentation, and pasted information. If there is no pasted text yet, it scans another mover. Pages are fetched again when older than five minutes. Your manual request takes priority for at least 35 seconds; an open reader pauses rotation. Pause/resume controls the scan cycle, and a separate motion button controls animation. Visible-tab market refresh continues when the cycle is paused. Closing or hiding the tab stops automatic work.

Data & feedback

The movers feed is a discovery sample from DEX Screener profiles, boosts and pump.fun / PumpSwap search results. New candidates are discovered each minute; their market data refreshes every 15 seconds. The most liquid pair per token is shown. Market feedback describes reported momentum, transactions, liquidity and volatility. It does not evaluate holder concentration, authorities or contract security, and does not recommend trades.

Privacy & sources

Token CAs go to DEX Screener; URLs and searches go to Jina Reader and DuckDuckGo. Source previews load the external site directly. Pasted text analysis stays in your browser. Your last scanned CA and up to 20 public pages are saved in local browser storage. Avoid entering private links or wallet secrets. No wallet connection or trading is required.
